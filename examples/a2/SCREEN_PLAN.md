# A₂ perturbation screen

Consider the exact truncated family (no unspecified higher input):
`U_t=V_x`,
`V_t=3UU_x+ε²[A(U)U_xxx+B(U)U_xx U_x+C(U)U_x³]`,
on a connected real interval with `U>0). Use the canonical coordinate
and quasi-Miura conventions of the A₂ example.

1. Construct the equation-only `G₂` with the coupled hodograph
   quadratures. Transport a nontrivial admissible leading symmetry and
   derive necessary differential equations from the negative powers in
   the physical jets. Use independent coefficient jets; point sampling
   is not a proof.
2. Screen the surviving branches at the next required order. A failed
   representation of the quasi-Miura map is inconclusive by itself.
   Require a certified nonpolynomial coefficient or an independent
   incompatibility of the polynomial symmetry equations.
3. Solve the resulting differential conditions under
   `A=c₁U^m₁, B=c₂U^m₂, C=c₃U^m₃` for real exponents, handling zero
   amplitudes without dividing by them. Distinguish finite necessary
   conditions from sufficient or all-order results.
4. Verify each surviving branch using the full generic Tsarev family,
   or prove it is a rescaling/specialization of a known hierarchy.
   Confirm that the original `(1/4,0,0)` is recovered, and include an
   explicit nonmember that fails the screen.
